The Sacred Journey

सप्तपदी — The Seven Sacred Steps

Saptapadi — the seven sacred vows — is the heart of a Maharashtrian wedding. With each step around the sacred fire, Mohanish and Shivani pledge eternal love, trust, and companionship.

Step One 🌾 प्रथम पद Nourishment

We shall nourish each other and all beings, sharing in abundance and gratitude.

Step Two 💪 द्वितीय पद Strength

We shall stand strong together through all of life's trials, becoming each other's shield.

Step Three 🌟 तृतीय पद Prosperity

We shall share all wealth, striving for spiritual and material prosperity together.

Step Four 🌸 चतुर्थ पद Happiness

We shall fill our home with joy, love, laughter, and mutual contentment.

Step Five 🐄 पञ्चम पद Progeny

We pray for noble children, nurturing them with wisdom, virtue, and devotion.

Step Six 🌿 षष्ठ पद Longevity

We shall lead a long, healthy life together, offering service to all beings.

Step Seven 🕉️ सप्तम पद Eternal Love

We shall be lifelong companions — faithful, devoted, and inseparable in all things.
